This study introduces NanoSYNMAP, a genetic, optical and computational platform that combines FRET with synaptome mapping to measure nanoscale protein proximity within individual synapses across the whole mouse brain. Using it, the authors build the first brainwide atlas of synaptic nanoarchitecture, based on the proximity of postsynaptic MAGUK supercomplexes. Nanoarchitecture differs between brain regions, differentiates during postnatal development and remodels with ageing, and deletion of a neuropsychiatric risk gene triggers widespread reorganisation of synaptic nanoscale architecture.
