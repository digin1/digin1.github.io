Traditional effect size measures such as Cohen's d focus on differences in means and overlook differences in variance between groups. This preprint proposes the overlap area (OA) between the population distributions of experimental and control groups as an effect size measure, estimated with a Bayesian approach, with significance thresholds for single and replicated experiments set by random resampling. Simulations support the method's sensitivity and robustness, and applied to a real dataset OA reveals the effects of environmental enrichment on the mouse brain synaptome. An open-source toolbox for OA analyses is released alongside the paper.
