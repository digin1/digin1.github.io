Privacy

Cookies and analytics

This is a personal website. It doesn't have accounts, adverts or tracking for marketing.

Analytics (only if you accept)

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics to count page views and see roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_*, which last up to two years. IP addresses are anonymised.

If you click Reject, or don't choose, Google Analytics isn't loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

What is stored on your device

  • cookie-consent in local storage: remembers your Accept or Reject choice.
  • theme in local storage: remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.

Neither of these is sent anywhere.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered by Formspree and used only to reply to you.

Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time. Withdrawing consent stops analytics and deletes its cookies from this site.
