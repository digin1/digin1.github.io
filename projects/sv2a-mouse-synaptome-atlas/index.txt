2c:T1767,<h2 id="project-overview">Project Overview</h2>
<p>The SV2A Mouse Synaptome Atlas is a modern web application for visualizing research data on Synaptic Vesicle Glycoprotein 2A (SV2A) protein diversity across the mouse brain. The platform enables researchers to explore how synaptic vesicle protein density and intensity vary across brain regions, developmental stages, and between sexes through an interactive brain atlas interface.</p>
<h2 id="project-details">Project Details</h2>
<h3>Technologies Used</h3>
<ul>
<li>Next.js 14 (App Router)</li>
<li>TypeScript</li>
<li>React 18</li>
<li>Tailwind CSS</li>
<li>SVG Manipulation</li>
<li>Allen Brain Atlas Integration</li>
<li>GitLab CI/CD</li>
<li>Static Site Generation</li>
</ul>
<h3>Key Features</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Interactive Brain Atlas</strong>: SVG-based mouse brain visualization with clickable regions, color-coded by SV2A measurements</li>
<li><strong>Dual Metric Analysis</strong>: Toggle between SV2A density and intensity measurements</li>
<li><strong>Sex Differences Analysis</strong>: View data for male, female, or combined cohorts to reveal sex-specific patterns</li>
<li><strong>Multi-Age Comparison</strong>: Compare data across 3 developmental stages (6 weeks, 6 months, 18 months)</li>
<li><strong>Region Search</strong>: Autocomplete search functionality to quickly find specific brain regions</li>
<li><strong>Dynamic Color Mapping</strong>: Real-time color scale visualization based on selected metric and filters</li>
<li><strong>Regional Data Table</strong>: Sortable list of brain regions with measurement values</li>
<li><strong>Selected Region Details</strong>: Floating panel showing comprehensive data across all conditions</li>
<li><strong>Dataset Download</strong>: Excel file export of complete research data</li>
<li><strong>Methodology Documentation</strong>: Detailed research methodology and data analysis procedures</li>
<li><strong>Responsive Design</strong>: Full mobile/tablet support with optimized touch controls</li>
</ul>
<h3>Development Process</h3>
<p>Developed as the web developer for the Grant Lab at University of Edinburgh to present their synaptic vesicle protein research in an accessible, interactive format.</p>
<h4>Frontend Architecture</h4>
<p>Built with Next.js 14 App Router and TypeScript:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Component Structure</strong>:
<ul>
<li><code>BrainAtlas.tsx</code> - Interactive SVG brain visualization with dynamic coloring</li>
<li><code>ControlPanel.tsx</code> - Metric, age, and sex selection controls</li>
